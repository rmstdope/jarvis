Click a field to edit it — every state

Agreed so far: click (or Tab to it and press Enter) the title, owner or goal to change just that one. Clicking away or Enter saves; Esc puts it back. In the goal, Enter is a new line, and Save, Cancel or ⌘↵ saves. Closed initiatives can be edited too. A save is confirmed quietly. Every word on this page is a proposal — check them.
