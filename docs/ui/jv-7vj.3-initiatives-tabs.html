jv-7vj.3 — initiatives as tabs (round 2)

From the Initiatives page, Browse & search, a task's “◎” label or line, or the agent. Badge “Initiative”, status pill, Close initiative and Ask about this in the header; owner and dates on the meta line; progress and goal beneath.
