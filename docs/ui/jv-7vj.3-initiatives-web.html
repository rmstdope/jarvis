12. Keyboard, focus and what persists


• The Initiatives page opens with no cursor (no search box); every card, filter, task checkbox, label, Accept, Show all, Close initiative and Reopen is reachable by Tab. An initiative page opens at the top.
• Close initiative's question opens with focus on Cancel; Esc or Cancel closes it and focus returns to Close initiative. After closing, focus moves to Reopen, and back to Close initiative after reopening.
• Accept: focus moves to the next suggestion (or the Suggested heading when none are left).
• Across a reload: the Open / Closed / All and owner filters are kept. Coming back by the trail restores the list as left. "Show all" is not remembered.
• New data: the page shows what was there when it opened; progress changes at once when you tick a task here.
