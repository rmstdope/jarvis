17 · Every word

Card in the answer: the title, then “<kind> · opened in a tab”. Dot on a tab, on hover: “Opened by the agent”.
Tab: hover shows the full title, then “Ctrl+Tab or Ctrl+Alt+→ next tab · Ctrl+Shift+Tab or Ctrl+Alt+← previous tab” · ✕ hover “Close tab” · overflow “⌄ <n> more” · phone button “▤ <n>”, hover “Open tabs”.
Kinds (badge): “Note” · “Task” · “Web page” · “Video transcript” · “Meeting transcript” · “PDF” · “Briefing” · “Presentation”.
Header: “Ask about this” · presentations “Present” and, when the team’s deck app is running, “Present in deck viewer” (as today) · briefings show their status (“draft” / “approved”).
Slides: “‹” “›” hovers “Previous slide” / “Next slide” · “<i> / <n>” · in Present, “Esc to leave”.
PDF: “Text taken from <file name> — the original layout and images aren’t kept.” · “Open the original ↗” (web PDFs only).
Note: “Links (<n>)”. Briefing: “Contents” · “Comments (<n>)” · “Add a comment…” · “Post” (as today).
Opening: “Opening…” · Gone: “This document isn’t here any more.” / “It may have been removed, or renamed by a sync.” / “Close tab” · Failed: “Couldn’t open this document.” / “Something went wrong while loading it.” / “Try again” / “Close tab”.
Keyboard: Tab reaches the strip; ←/→ move between tabs, Enter shows one, Delete closes the focused one. Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+Shift+Tab (where the browser lets them through, e.g. the Mac app) and Ctrl+Alt+→/← (everywhere) go to the next / previous tab. In a presentation ←/→ change slides; Esc leaves Present.
Empty: “Nothing written here yet.” · “This presentation has no slides yet.” · “This slide couldn’t be shown.”
