On-call rollout: decisions from Tuesday

note · Anna Berg · 4 Nov 2026
Weekly on-call rotation from 1 December; escalation to the platform lead after 30 minutes; every paging alert needs a runbook.

We met on Tuesday to settle how the new on-call rotation will work. Everyone agreed the current two-week rotation is too long and that escalation is unclear at night.

Decisions

  • Weekly rotation from 1 December, handing over on Mondays at 10:00.
  • After 30 minutes without acknowledgement, the page escalates to the platform lead.
  • Every alert must link to a runbook before it may page anyone.

Open questions

Whether weekend shifts count double towards time off is still with HR.

5. A briefing on a tablet-width window

the side columns tuck away by themselves

On a tablet-width window (the sidebar is already a rail there) both side columns start hidden as slim strips, whatever you chose on a wide screen. Opening one slides it over the text from its side; ✕ or a click on the text closes it. Your wide-screen choice comes back when the window widens. On a phone the strips become the two buttons beside the title.
